Full Rome rules
Founded on the banks of the Tiber, the city of Rome is the political center of the republic as well as a symbol of its glorious power. Here, the Senate hold council, citizens gather in public, and generals walk the streets in triumph, returning with newfound glory for Rome. Capture Rome, and you shall control the fate of its future. Declare your ambitions against all the provinces and legions as you fight for supremacy. 1. The city of Rome will open for contention every 3 days. During this period, all coalitions with active territory connected to Rome can attack it. Any coalition that can capture and hold Rome for 8 hours will gain control of it. 2. The first commander (or captain of the rallied army) to occupy the city of Rome will become the defense captain responsible for repelling enemy attacks. If this commander is withdrawn, the commander with the highest power will automatically succeed the position. 3. All units who are severely wounded during battles for Rome will die immediately (during battles with barbarians, all severely wounded units will be sent to the hospital).

Declaration, contention and ladders
The towering wall is a haven for the bustling cultures within, but also a target for ambitious conquerors. When the Legion's eagles arrive, the city's fate shall be rewritten in blood. Declaration and Contention 1. To declare war on a Civitas, the declaring side's alliance territory must be connected to a Civitas gate. After war is declared, the Civitas will enter a 5-minute Preparation period (shortened to 1 minute if the Civitas is occupied by Barbarians). During Preparation, any alliances that declare war on the Civitas will participate in its subsequent contention. 2. Declaration Points are required to declare war. Declaration Points are held independently by each alliance and naturally recover over time. 3. The Contention period lasts 1 hour for Small Civitates and 2 hours for Large Civitates. 4. Only by destroying a Civitas's Forum can you successfully capture it. If the declaring side fails to destroy the Forum during Contention, all buildings in the Civitas will recover 15% Durability. 5. The declaring side's troops can enter the Civitas after destroying its gate, or enter by making use of ladders. Ladders 1. The declaring side can spend alliance resources to build ladders (maximum 4). The cooldown after each construction is 15 minutes. 2. Placing ladders at designated ladder locations near walls will allow troops to scale walls directly. 3. Ladders can be attacked and will be destroyed when Durability reaches 0. Other 1. After a Civitas has been captured, Teleporters and other buff-providing buildings can be built in specific locations. You may choose to build them according to your needs. 2. After an alliance captures a Civitas, the Civitas is considered as alliance territory and troops will have access to their alliance technology buffs when fighting in it.

Forum and final-hit capture rules
The towering Forum is the foundation of a Civitas. The alliance that successfully destroys it captures the entire Civitas. ◆ Declaring War 1. When alliance territory is connected to Civitas territory during Peace or Preparation, officers with permissions may declare war on the Civitas. 2. Declaring war on a Small Civitas costs {p1} War Decrees, and declaring war on a Large Civitas costs {p2} War Decrees. 3. An alliance can only declare war on 1 Civitas at a time. Multiple alliances can declare war on the same Civitas. 4. During periods of Peace, a Civitas enters Preparation immediately if war is declared on it. After Preparation ends, the Civitas will enter Contention. ◆ Contention 1. During the Contention period, Civitas buildings can only be attacked by alliances that previously declared war on the Civitas. Small Civitates have a Contention period of {p3} hours, and Large Civitates have a Contention period of {p4} hours. Alliances cannot switch coalitions during Contention. 2. During the Contention period, Forums can only be attacked after the Civitas gate is destroyed. When a Civitas's Forum is destroyed, the alliance of the troop that dealt the finishing blow will immediately capture the Civitas. The Civitas will enter Armistice after. 3. If the Forum is still standing by the time the Contention period ends, then the siege is considered to have failed. The Civitas will immediately enter Armistice. Alliances must redeclare war after Armistice ends to attack the Civitas again. ◆ Armistice 1. When a Civitas enters the Armistice period, destroyed gates will immediately recover some Durability and restore passage restrictions that prevent troops outside of the occupying alliance from entering. The Forum will also immediately recover some Durability. 2. During Armistice, other alliances cannot declare war on the Civitas, and its buildings cannot be attacked. 3. Small Civitates have an Armistice period of {p5} minutes, and Large Civitates have an Armistice period of {p6} minutes. If the Civitas' occupation status changes, the current Armistice period will be extended by {p7} minutes. ◆ Civitas Occupation During Armistice and Peace, the occupying alliance of a Civitas can build Teleporters, Aquilas, and other functional buildings to help prepare against siege.

Gate mechanics
The Civitas gate holds key passage for entry. Destroy it and attacking troops will be able to gain access. 1. When the gate's status is normal, only troops of the occupying alliance can enter the Civitas, though all troops can exit without issue. 2. When the gate's Durability is reduced to 0, it becomes destroyed and all troops can enter the Civitas through it. 3. When a gate is destroyed, any functional building behind it immediately becomes inactive. 4. When Civitates enters Armistice, destroyed gates will immediately return to normal, and only troops of the occupying alliance can enter again. 5. Only gates can serve as a the starting point of alliance territory and connect to alliance flags.
CITY_DOOR_INFO
Gate and territory connection
A heavy gate that safeguards the civitas's vital entry points. It is both a tough barrier against enemies, as well as a structure of heavy contention between warring camps. 1. To declare war on a Civitas, the declaring side's alliance territory must be connected to a Civitas gate. 2. When a Civitas gate is still intact, troops outside of the occupying coalition cannot enter through it. 3. After destroying a Civitas gate, all troops will be able to enter through it. 4. A gate's durability has no bearing on its occupation status. Whoever captures the Civitas will occupy its gate.

Wall mechanics
The wall is a Civitas's bulwark against siege. Troops that scale walls can receive combat buffs. 1. All troops on walls can receive wall buffs, regardless of alliance affiliation. 2. Troops of the attacking side can scale walls using ladders. 3. Ranged troops in Civitates can only attack troops outside when they're on walls.
CITY_WALL_INFO
Ladder capacity, placement and rebuilding
Even the sturdiest walls can be breached. 1. After Contention begins, the attacking alliance can build ladders on alliance territory adjacent to the Civitas it declared war on. Each alliance can have up to {p1} ladders at a time. 2. Ladders can only be placed at designated ladder locations. Troops on ladders will be able to scale walls. 3. Each ladder can hold up to {p2} troops at a time. 4. When a ladder is successfully placed at a ladder location, all troops on the ladder will simultaneously climb on top of the wall. Any subsequent troops on the ladder will require {p3} seconds to reach the wall. 5. When a ladder's Durability is reduced to 0, it becomes destroyed. You must wait {p4} minutes after a ladder is destroyed to rebuild one.

Ladder location
Even the sturdiest walls can be breached. 1. Ladders can only be placed at designated ladder locations. Troops on ladders will be able to scale walls. 2. The ladder captain can command the ladder to march towards ladder locations. Once in position, the ladder will automatically begin placement.

Teleporter and routed troops
Teleporters are important functional buildings in the Civitas that can quickly provide battle reinforcements. 1. A Civitas's occupying coalition can send troops into the Civitas via Teleporters. There is no limit on how many troops can be sent at once. 2. Only troops inside governor cities can be sent through Teleporters. Troops on the map cannot. 3. Troops inside the Civitas can return to their respective cities through the Teleporter. 4. When a Teleporter becomes inactive, it can no longer teleport troops. 5. After a Teleporter becomes inactive or is destroyed, troops sent through this Teleporter will march towards their city when routed. 6. When a Teleporter belongs to a neutral side, troops from that side can send reinforcements to the battlefield through the Teleporter.
CITY_TP_INFO
Aquila mechanics
The Aquila stands tall within the city despite the chaos of surrounding war. Symbolizes an unflinching promise to hold the lines and protect one's allies. 1. Aquilas can provide buffs to the occupying coalition of a Civitas. 2. When an Aquila becomes inactive, it can no longer provide buffs.

Barbarian-controlled Civitas
Defeat the barbarians and take back the Civitas! 1. Attacking a Civitas captured by Barbarians will not cost Action Points. 2. When a Civitas is being contended, garrisoned Barbarians will automatically attack any approaching troops. 3. When Barbarian troops start attacking, the Teleporter within the Civitas will send reinforcements. 4. When a Civitas is under periods of Peace or Armistice, you won't be able to attack Barbarian troops inside it.

Full strategy rules
The Aquila soars as Legions clash. The fate of Rome shall hence be decided. In this season, you can use Strategies to bolster your conquest. Adapt to the shifting tides of the battlefield and deploy Strategies accordingly, and you shall emerge victorious. 1. The Strategies of this season are divided into three branches: Siege, Field, and Development. Each branch has 4 levels: Basic, Intermediate, Advanced, and Ultimate. Within the same branch, you must adopt low-level strategies before moving onto higher-level ones. 2. You can only choose one Strategy from each level within the same branch. 3. Different Strategies cost different amounts of Strategy Points to adopt. Basic Strategies cost 1, Intermediate Strategies cost 2, Advanced Strategies cost 3, and Ultimate Strategies cost 4 Strategy Points. 4. Strategies can be reset at the cost of a small amount of Crystals. You are encouraged to explore various strategy combinations. 5. In the March of the Legions seasonal story, you can earn up to 16 Strategy Points: 12 from the Lost Kingdom Chronicles and the rest from Crusader Achievements and the Midseason Review event. 6. All Strategies are only available in this Lost Kingdom.

Barbarian battle rules
Defeat the barbarians and take back the Civitas! 1. Attacking a Civitas captured by Barbarians will not cost Action Points. 2. When a Civitas is being contended, garrisoned Barbarians will automatically attack any approaching troops. 3. When Barbarian troops start attacking, the Teleporter within the Civitas will send reinforcements. 4. When a Civitas is under periods of Peace or Armistice, you won't be able to attack Barbarian troops inside it.

Artifact & early access

Seasonal artifact, story rules, rewards and tutorial text.

Knot of Serpentine Fate

The Knot of Serpentine Fate unlocks at a certain point in the Lost Kingdom Chronicles, and all governors can obtain this artifact. If Prime Julius Caesar or Prime Cleopatra VII has this artifact equipped and is serving as secondary commander, the artifact will take effect.

Exact artifact effect is not present in the extracted description.

Why offer early access?
Although the core content of this story is now complete, as part of a new season there may be areas that still require improvement. With early access, we hope governors can get a sneak peek while providing us with feedback to create a more enjoyable story. Early access is planned to be an infrequent event.
SIGN_EARLY_ACCESS_QA_A_S22_1
What are the sorts of things we'll get to see in this story?
In the March of the Legions story, you can experience siege battles in cities of Rome (Civitates), as well as new Seasonal Strategies. Civitates: In this season, lead your troops across the map to conquer one Civitas after another. Scale city walls with ladders, and work with or against other governors to fight for control of each Civitas. Strategies: A new strategy system is available for this season. Select different strategies as you progress through the season to speed up your efforts.
SIGN_EARLY_ACCESS_QA_A_S22_2
What is the schedule of this new story?
In the March of the Legions story, the battle schedule will be roughly as follows (the specific days are subject to change and are only for reference): On day 9, governors from different camps will meet each other for the first time. On day 27, all camps will begin intense siege battles over Civitates. On day 29, the final battle for the King's Land and the capture of Rome will begin.
SIGN_EARLY_ACCESS_QA_A_S22_3
Where can I give suggestions or feedback?
You can share any thoughts or issues with us by going to Settings -> Customer Support. We pay close attention to all feedback we receive and will send you an in-game reward afterwards.
SIGN_EARLY_ACCESS_QA_A_S22_4
Will I receive a special reward for taking part?
Governors who take part in the newest story will be eligible for early access rewards and participation rewards, in addition to regular story rewards.

Story introduction
The die is cast, and Rome is engulfed in the flames of civil war. Allies who once stood alongside the Legion's eagle now raise their blades against it, as vows of the past shatter beneath the roar of battle drums. From the city's high walls to its winding alleys, every inch of Rome must be conquered with blood. Fight along with trusted allies, make use of talented commanders, and switch between offensive and defensive tactics. Let cities welcome their new rulers amid the unceasing waves of conquest. The path of conquest ends with Rome. This ancient city shall become your final battleground, and the Legion's blade the final judge of victory. When the dust settles and the Eagle finds its mark, who shall command the fate of Rome in the name of Caesar?
